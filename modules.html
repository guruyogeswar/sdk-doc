Brain modules

Thirty independent units of logic, each loosely named after a brain region or cognitive function. Modules read pools, write pools, and never import each other. The orchestrator decides which ones run, and in what order, based on the active preset.

Which modules run

Affective & motivational 6 modules

amygdala_module
Threat detection and emotional salience tagging.
hypothalamus_module
Homeostatic drives — hunger, thirst, fatigue.
interoception_module
Body-state sensing fed back to higher modules.
neuromodulator_module
DA, 5-HT, NE, ACh, cortisol, oxytocin, endorphins.
learned_drive_module
Acquired motivations beyond hardwired drives.
behavioral_regulation_module
Impulse control and inhibition.

Memory & learning 6 modules

hippocampus_module
Episodic memory: encode, consolidate, retrieve.
learning_module
Temporal-difference reinforcement learning.
plasticity_module
Hebbian and dopamine-gated synaptic updates.
meta_learning_module
Adapts learning rate from outcome variance.
causal_model_module
Cause → effect belief graph with confidence.
world_model_module
Forward model used by planning and active inference.

Attention & control 6 modules

acc_module
Anterior cingulate — conflict and error monitoring.
attentional_dynamics_module
Gain control over sensory and memory channels.
pfc_module
Prefrontal cortex — top-down executive bias on action selection.
planning_module
Short-horizon rollouts over the world model.
active_inference_module
Free-energy minimisation between belief and observation.
metacognition_module
Confidence estimation over the agent's own decisions.

Action selection 4 modules

basal_ganglia_module
D1 Go / D2 NoGo arbitration over 28 action types.
action_execution_module
Commits the selected action into the decision pool.
expression_module
Translates internal state to externally-readable cues.
cognitive_trace_module
Logs per-tick decision evidence for explain().

Social, symbolic & self 9 modules

theory_of_mind_module
Models other agents' beliefs and intents.
social_cognition_module
Trust, alliance, betrayal — per-pair edges.
identity_module
Evolved personality vector — trust, optimism, aggression, stability.
narrative_module
Encodes the agent's life story from episodic memories.
narrative_self_module
Maintains a self-model coherent with the narrative.
coherence_module
Enforces internal consistency across belief, mood, identity.
symbolic_reasoner_module
Light logical inference over symbolic propositions.
symbolic_grounding_module
Bridges sensorimotor state to symbols.
evolution_economy_module
Multi-agent population dynamics and resource flow.
